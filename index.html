StepBoard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

StepBoard is a visual First-Then board for children, set up and supervised by a parent, teacher, or other adult. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect your data, we have no servers, and we do not track you.

Information stored on your device

Boards, cards, schedules, and routines you create are saved on your device. This includes any photos, videos, or audio you add to cards. We, the developer, never receive this content.

iCloud sync and group sharing

If you use iCloud sync or share a group with other people, your data is stored in Apple's iCloud using CloudKit, in your own iCloud account. It is protected by your Apple ID and Apple's security. Shared groups are visible only to the people you invite. We cannot see or access this data.

Camera, photos, and microphone

The camera and photo library are used only to create cards and to scan a posted schedule. The microphone is used only to record sound for video cards and for spoken setup. This content stays on your device or in your iCloud account.

Schedule scanning

When you scan a schedule, text is recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The image is not sent to us or to any third party.

Voice setup

Voice setup uses Apple's speech recognition. The app asks for on-device recognition when your device supports it. If your device does not, Apple may process the audio under Apple's own privacy policy. We never receive the audio or the transcript.

Face ID

Face ID or your device passcode is used only to confirm that an adult is leaving a locked board. The app never receives or stores your biometric data.

Notifications

If you allow them, notifications are used for routine reminders and to keep shared groups up to date.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

StepBoard contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children's privacy

StepBoard is intended to be set up and managed by an adult. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, or from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. You can remove iCloud data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
