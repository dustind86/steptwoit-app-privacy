StepBoard: First-Then Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

StepBoard: First-Then is a visual First-Then board for children, set up and supervised by a parent, teacher, or other adult. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect your data, we have no servers, and we do not track you. Everything stays on your device unless you choose to share a group with other people.

Information stored on your device

Boards, cards, schedules, routines, stickers, and progress you create are saved on your device. This includes any photos, videos, or audio you add to cards, and the answers to mood check-ins. We, the developer, never receive this content.

Mood check-ins

Answers to the mood check-in are stored only on your device. They are not synced to iCloud and are not shared with anyone, even when a group is shared.

Sharing a group

Nothing is uploaded to iCloud until you share a group. Sharing is optional. If you share a group, the group's data is stored in Apple's iCloud using CloudKit, and it becomes visible to the people you invite. A shared group includes:

  • the group's name and each participant's role (owner, editor, support, or viewer);
  • each student's name, avatar, and board settings;
  • boards, routines, and the progress and stickers a student has earned (including dates and routine names);
  • schedules, including times and any text from scanned schedules, and special days such as birthdays;
  • cards, including any photos and videos attached to them.

Only share a group with people you trust with this information. The group owner can see who has joined through Apple's sharing screen, which may show their Apple ID name, email address, or phone number, and can choose each person's role or remove them. Data in a shared group is stored in iCloud under Apple's privacy policy and security. We cannot see or access it.

Camera, photos, and microphone

The camera is used only to take photos for cards and to scan a posted schedule. When you add a photo or video from your library, the app receives only the items you choose. The microphone is used only to record sound for video cards and for spoken setup. This content stays on your device, or in your iCloud account if you share a group that includes it.

Photo cutouts

When you remove a photo's background to make a card or sticker, this is done on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The photo is not sent anywhere.

Schedule scanning

When you scan a schedule, text is recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The image is not sent to us or to any third party.

Voice setup

Voice setup uses Apple's speech recognition. The app asks for on-device recognition when your device supports it. If your device does not, Apple may process the audio under Apple's own privacy policy. We never receive the audio or the transcript.

Face ID

Face ID or your device passcode is used only to confirm that an adult is leaving a locked board. The app never receives or stores your biometric data.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app shows reminders for routines at the times you schedule. These are created on your device. The app also receives silent background notifications from Apple, which are not shown to you, so that changes to a shared group appear quickly.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

StepBoard: First-Then contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children's privacy

The app is intended to be set up and managed by an adult. We do not collect any personal information from children, or from anyone. If an adult adds a child's name, photo, or other details to the app, that information stays on the device unless the adult shares the group, as described above.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. Deleting a group you own removes its shared data from iCloud. You can also remove iCloud data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
